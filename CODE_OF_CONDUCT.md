# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.
Contributors must communicate constructively, respect privacy and consent, avoid
personal attacks or discriminatory conduct, and keep technical disagreement
focused on evidence and project goals.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, doxxing, publishing private
information, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit,
reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community.

Before public launch, the owner must publish a private enforcement contact and
adopt a counsel-reviewed full community policy. Until then, concerns should be
raised privately with the repository owner and should contain no secrets or
private project data.
